PassGuardian deployment notes

1. Set Vercel backend environment variables:
   NODE_ENV=production
   CLIENT_URL=https://pass-gaurdian-12.vercel.app,http://localhost:5173
   MONGODB_URI=<your rotated MongoDB URI>
   JWT_SECRET=<your rotated JWT secret>
   ENCRYPTION_KEY=<your encryption key>
   SMTP_HOST=smtp.gmail.com
   SMTP_PORT=587
   SMTP_USER=<your email>
   SMTP_PASS=<your rotated Gmail app password>
   EMAIL_FROM=PassGuardian <your email>

2. Frontend:
   VITE_API_BASE_URL=https://pass-gaurdian.vercel.app/api/v1

3. Redeploy after changing Vercel environment variables.

Do not commit real credentials to source control.
